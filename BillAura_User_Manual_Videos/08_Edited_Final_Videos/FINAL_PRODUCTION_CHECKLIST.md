# Bill Aura ERP — Final Edited Videos Production Checklist

This checklist governs the post-production assembly, audio mastering, subtitle alignment, color grading, and final packaging for the video manual library.

---

## 1. Post-Production Standards
- [ ] **Intro Stinger**: 2.5-second elegant Bill Aura animation with subtle audio branding chime.
- [ ] **Video Codec**: MP4 H.264 High Profile, Level 4.2. Variable Bit Rate (VBR) 2-pass, Target 12 Mbps, Max 16 Mbps.
- [ ] **Audio Mix**: EBU R128 / ITU-R BS.1770-4 compliance. Integrated loudness target: -16.0 LUFS (+/- 0.5 LUFS), Max True Peak: -1.0 dBFS.
- [ ] **Subtitles**: Hardcoded burned-in subtitles optional; primary delivery as separate `.srt` sidecar files in UTF-8 encoding.
- [ ] **Callout Banners**: Semi-transparent dark navy (`#0F172A` at 85% opacity) with gold border (`#F59E0B`), Inter font, 24pt legible typography.
- [ ] **Outro Card**: 5-second end screen displaying "Next Chapter", official support email (`support@billaura.com`), and documentation link.
