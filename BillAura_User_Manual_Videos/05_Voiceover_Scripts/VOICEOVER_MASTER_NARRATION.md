# Bill Aura ERP — Voiceover Master Narration & Audio Direction

## 1. Voice Talent & Acoustic Direction
- **Voice Profile**: Professional, confident, warm, authoritative, calm, and articulate.
- **Language / Accent**: Standard Indian English (clear, neutral corporate tone, easily understood across all Indian business hubs and international audiences).
- **Target Pace**: 140 words per minute. Allow natural 0.8s micro-pauses between major sentences to permit the viewer to visually locate UI controls.
- **Microphone & Processing**: Cardioid condenser microphone (e.g. Shure SM7B or Rode NT1), 24-bit 48kHz WAV recording. Moderate compression (3:1 ratio, 20ms attack, 100ms release), subtle high-pass filter at 80Hz, gentle de-esser. Peak normalization to -1.0 dBFS, overall loudness normalized to -16 LUFS.

## 2. Indian Financial & Technical Pronunciation Guide

| Term | Phonetic Guide | Context & Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **GSTIN** | *G-S-T-I-N* (spell out letters individually: jee-ess-tee-eye-en) | Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (15 digits) |
| **HSN** | *H-S-N* (aitch-ess-en) | Harmonized System of Nomenclature (4, 6, or 8 digits) |
| **SAC** | *S-A-C* (ess-ay-see) | Services Accounting Code |
| **CGST** | *C-G-S-T* (see-jee-ess-tee) | Central Goods and Services Tax |
| **SGST** | *S-G-S-T* (ess-jee-ess-tee) | State Goods and Services Tax |
| **IGST** | *I-G-S-T* (eye-jee-ess-tee) | Integrated Goods and Services Tax (Inter-state transactions) |
| **TDS** | *T-D-S* (tee-dee-ess) | Tax Deducted at Source |
| **ITC** | *I-T-C* (eye-tee-see) | Input Tax Credit |
| **RCM** | *R-C-M* (ahr-see-em) | Reverse Charge Mechanism |
| **UPI** | *U-P-I* (yoo-pee-eye) | Unified Payments Interface |
| **UTR** | *U-T-R* (yoo-tee-ahr) | Unique Transaction Reference (Bank NEFT/RTGS/IMPS code) |
| **COGS** | *Cogs* (rhymes with logs) or *C-O-G-S* | Cost of Goods Sold |
| **EBITDA** | *ee-bit-dah* | Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization |
| **Paise** | *pie-say* | Fractional Indian currency unit (100 paise = 1 Rupee) |
| **Rupee (₹)** | *roo-pee* | Official currency of India |

### Track 09: Sales Invoicing & GST Taxation (Time: 14:30)
*(Tone: Precise, authoritative, instructional)*
"Sales Invoicing is the revenue engine of your business. In an Indian statutory context, a sales invoice establishes your GST output liability, decrements inventory stock, populates your GSTR-1 return, and establishes your Accounts Receivable balance.

Let us create a comprehensive tax invoice. Click '+ New Invoice'. 

Select our customer, 'Apex Solutions LLP'. Because Apex Solutions is registered in Maharashtra and our company is registered in Maharashtra, the Place of Supply automatically defaults to State Code 27. This instructs Bill Aura to apply Central GST and State GST.

Now, let us add line items:
We select 'Precision Ball Bearing 6204'. Notice the HSN code 8482 populates automatically. We input twenty-five pieces at four hundred and fifty rupees, applying a two percent trade discount.

Notice the Auto Round-Off feature. The unrounded sum is twenty-six thousand, two hundred and twenty-five rupees and fifty paise. Bill Aura automatically adds a fifty-paise adjustment to bring the final payable amount to an exact twenty-six thousand, two hundred and twenty-six rupees.

Click 'Save and Issue'. In that single instant, stock is decremented in your warehouse, accounts receivable is debited, sales revenue is credited, and statutory GST output liability is posted to your tax accounts."
