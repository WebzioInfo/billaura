- **Target Audience**: Business owners, CEOs, CFOs, operations directors, branch heads.

---

## Instructional Sequence

### Section 1: Introduction (00:00 – 01:15)
- **On-Screen**: `ExecutiveDashboard.tsx` (`/dashboard`). Full HD view.
- **Narration**:
  "Welcome back to Bill Aura ERP. The Executive Dashboard serves as your business command center. The moment you log into Bill Aura, you are presented with a real-time, consolidated pulse of your enterprise's financial health, cash liquidity, sales performance, and upcoming liabilities.
  
  In this chapter, you will learn how each metric is computed directly from your underlying accounting ledgers, how to filter views across date ranges and branches, and how to utilize quick-action widgets to initiate high-frequency tasks."

---

### Section 2: Top KPI Metric Cards (01:15 – 03:30)
- **On-Screen**: Zoom in (125%) to the top row of KPI metric cards.
- **Cursor Action**: Cursor hovers over Total Receivables, Total Payables, Cash & Bank Balance, and Net Profit.
- **Narration**:
  "Across the top row are four foundational financial indicators:
  
  1. **Total Receivables (Current Accounts Receivable)**: This card displays the total money owed to your company by customers across all open and overdue sales invoices. Below the main figure, notice the aging breakdown showing amounts due today versus amounts overdue by more than thirty days. Clicking this card opens the AR Aging Summary report directly.
  
  2. **Total Payables (Accounts Payable)**: This represents your outstanding liabilities to suppliers and vendors for bills received. It highlights bills coming due within the next seven days, allowing you to plan vendor disbursements smoothly without incurring late payment penalties or straining supplier relationships.
  
  3. **Cash & Bank Balances (Available Liquidity)**: This aggregate figure sums all real-time ledger balances across your operational current accounts, savings accounts, and petty cash registers. It provides instant visibility into your immediate cash reserves.
  
  4. **Net Profit for the Period**: Unlike a simplistic revenue total, this metric dynamically computes your Total Sales Revenue minus your Cost of Goods Sold and Operating Expenses up to this moment. It tells you exactly how profitable your business operations have been over the selected period."

---

### Section 3: Revenue & Expense Analytical Charts (03:30 – 05:15)
- **On-Screen**: Cursor pans down to the central chart widgets (Income vs. Expense bar graph, and Cash Flow line chart).
- **Narration**:
  "In the center of the dashboard are your trend visualization graphs powered by live accounting entries:
  
  The **Income vs Expense Graph** plots your monthly billing against business expenditures over the past six months. Hovering your mouse over any bar displays the exact revenue booked, expenses incurred, and the resulting monthly margin. Notice the seasonal trend lines that help you forecast capital requirements.
  
  Beside it is the **Cash Flow Statement Preview**. This line chart tracks actual cash inflows from customer receipts against cash outflows for vendor payments and operational expenses. If your cash flow line approaches zero, the dashboard visually alerts you before a liquidity crunch impacts operations."

---

### Section 4: Recent Transactions & Account Watchlist (05:15 – 06:45)
- **On-Screen**: Scroll down to the bottom widgets: 'Recent Activity & Transactions' table and 'Bank Account Watchlist'.
- **Narration**:
  "Lower on the dashboard, the **Recent Transactions Table** streams real-time updates as your staff operates across branches: invoices generated, payments logged, purchase orders approved, and stock adjustments completed. Clicking any transaction row immediately opens that voucher in a workspace tab.
  
  To the right is your **Bank Account Watchlist**. It displays each connected bank account with its account number, IFSC code, and current book balance. Clicking 'Reconcile' on any bank row transports you directly to the Bank Reconciliation Center."

---

### Section 5: Date Filters, Branch Filters & Recovery (06:45 – 08:15)
- **On-Screen**: Top-right corner of the dashboard. Cursor selects the Date Range picker (`This Financial Year`, `This Quarter`, `This Month`, `Custom Range`).
- **Narration**:
  "In the top-right corner, you can alter the analytical lens of your dashboard using the Date Range Selector. Switch from 'This Month' to 'This Financial Year' or choose a custom date range. Every KPI card, chart, and ledger calculation updates instantly.
  
  If your user account has multi-branch permissions, you can use the Branch Filter dropdown to view company-wide consolidated metrics or isolate a single warehouse or regional office.
  
  If a card appears blank or shows zero balances on a fresh installation, this is normal until your first transactions are recorded. As we progress through upcoming chapters, you will see these cards populate with rich, actionable intelligence.
  
  In Chapter 4, we dive into your product catalog, units of measure, and the Items Master."
